NMK media licensing and attribution

Media published by the National Museums of Kenya is available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), unless a different licence is shown on the media record.

What you may do

You may share, copy, remix, transform, and build upon the media for any purpose, including commercial purposes.

Attribution requirements

Give appropriate credit to the National Museums of Kenya, link to the source media or accession page, link to the CC BY 4.0 licence, and indicate whether changes were made.

  • Media title or description
  • © National Museums of Kenya
  • Source media or accession link
  • Licensed under CC BY 4.0
  • Note any changes

Example

“Fossilized skull of a prehistoric mammal”
© National Museums of Kenya
Source: NMK accession page
Licensed under CC BY 4.0

Branding

Attribution must not imply NMK endorsement. CC BY 4.0 does not grant permission to use NMK logos or branding.
