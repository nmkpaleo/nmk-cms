NMK media licensing and attribution

This media is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0).

You are free to

  • Share — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format.
  • Adapt — remix, transform, and build upon the material.

Licence terms

  • Attribution — credit the National Museums of Kenya, link to the licence, and indicate changes. Do not imply NMK endorsement.
  • NonCommercial — you may not use the material for commercial purposes.

Required attribution

  1. Title or description of the media.
  2. © National Museums of Kenya, or the named photographer and NMK.
  3. A link to the media or accession page.
  4. “Licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0” with a link to the licence.
  5. An indication of changes, if any.

Example attribution

“Fossilized skull of a prehistoric mammal”
© National Museums of Kenya
https://nmkpaleocms.co.ke/accessions/5/
Licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0

Where to place attribution

Place it near the media on websites and blogs, in social-media captions, in print captions or acknowledgements, and in video descriptions or on-screen credits.

Commercial use and branding

Commercial use, including advertisements, merchandise, and paid publications, requires prior permission from NMK. Please contact NMK to request permission. The licence does not grant permission to use NMK logos or branding.
